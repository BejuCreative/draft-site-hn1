About Dr. Judy Rosenberg

A psychologist on a mission to heal human disconnect.

Dr. Judy Rosenberg is a licensed clinical psychologist and the founder of the Psychological Healing Center. With more than 30 years of experience, she created the Be the Cause® Mind Map System and authored Be the Cause: Healing Human Disconnect.

Her specialty is getting to the root cause of symptoms rather than simply managing them. Those root causes are often part of a multigenerational blueprint passed down through families. Her mission: to heal human disconnect so we can heal global disconnect.
